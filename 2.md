Operators in C
Operators are symbols used to perform operations on values.

`Table of Oprator :-
~~~
|--------|--------|------------------|--------------------|
|        |Operator|	    Purpose	     |       Example      |
|--------|--------|------------------|--------------------| 
|   1    |    +   |   	Addition    	 |    a + b           |
|   2    |    -   |    Subtraction	  |    a - b           |
|   3    |    *   |   Multiplication	|    a * b           |
|   4    |    /   |    Division	     |    a / b           |
|   5    |    %	  |    Remainder	    |    a % b           |
|   6    |    ==	 |    Equal to	     |    a == b          |
|   7    |    >	  |    Greater than	 |    a > b           |  
|   8    |    &&	 |    Logical AND	  |    a > 0 && b > 0  |
|--------|--------|------------------|--------------------|
